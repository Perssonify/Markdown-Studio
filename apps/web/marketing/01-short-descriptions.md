## Elevator pitch — 300 characters

**Version A — the problem (282):**
> Markdown editors make you choose: write in syntax and imagine the result, or
> write in a rich editor and lose the plain-text file. Markdown Studio gives you
> both at once — two panes, one document, always in sync — with math, diagrams
> and linked notes. Free, private, in your browser.

**Version B — the capability (287):**
> Markdown Studio is a Markdown editor and a rich-text editor showing the same
> document at the same time. Add LaTeX, Mermaid diagrams, tables and task lists;
> link notes with `[[wiki links]]` and see them as a map; export to Markdown,
> HTML, Word or PDF. Your documents stay in your browser.
